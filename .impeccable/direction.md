# Gieo quẻ · Operate / Read

## Direction contract
THESIS: A quiet study desk: casting and reading share one continuous workspace.
OWN-WORLD: User confirmed dark, minimal and elegant. Warm ink, restrained aged brass, cinnabar moving lines, serif reading headings and clear sans controls.
STORY: Write an intention, cast six times, inspect the changing lines, then read the complete primary, changed and nuclear hexagrams.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim masthead, generous title, intention form on the left and a large bronze coin stage on the right. During casting the form becomes a six-line ledger. No emoji or decorative glow.
FORM: Code-led. User-pinned dark study overrides random visual alternatives (seed 300c0a06). Ruled ledger gives stable state positions; large controls retain the consumer interface accessibility discipline. No mascot or transport motifs.
SIGNATURE: Three individually staggered 3D coins rise, turn, land and settle; their final faces match the sampled score. Reduced motion resolves quickly without spinning.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
